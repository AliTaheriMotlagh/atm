How it works

A portfolio you drive

No scrolling through screenshots. Pull up to a building and the project opens, live.

  1. Drive the city

    WASD or the arrow keys, Shift to boost. On a phone, use the stick at the bottom left.

  2. Pull up to a landmark

    Each building is a project. Press E when you arrive, or click any landmark. M opens the map with GPS.

  3. Open its dossier

    The live website, source code, pitch, design notes, latest commits and three mini-games per project.

🏁 Goal: visit all 11 project landmarks, collect 8 gold coins, open 3 dossiers, and beat at least one challenge at every project.
